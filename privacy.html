Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Lucky's Weather handles information when you use this website.

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Information this website uses

Lucky's Weather does not require visitors to create an account or sign in. The website may display public weather information, public YouTube channel information, subscriber counts, videos, and livestream status.

YouTube information

The website uses YouTube API Services to determine whether the Lucky's Weather channel is live and to link viewers to public YouTube content. This feature uses read-only access to the channel owner's account. It does not allow the website to upload, edit, or delete videos, and it does not access a visitor's YouTube account.

Use of YouTube features is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Weather and third-party services

The website may load information or embedded content from third-party services, including the National Weather Service, NOAA, the Storm Prediction Center, the National Hurricane Center, YouTube, and SocialCounts. Those providers may process standard technical information under their own privacy policies.

Technical information

Website hosting, domain, and security providers such as GitHub Pages and Cloudflare may automatically process standard request information, including IP addresses, browser details, and security logs, to deliver and protect the website. Lucky's Weather does not sell visitors' personal information.

Cookies

Lucky's Weather does not intentionally place advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content may use cookies or similar technologies according to the provider's policies.

Data security and retention

Private credentials used to check the channel's livestream status are kept outside the public website code and are not shared with website visitors. Lucky's Weather does not intentionally retain personal information from ordinary website visitors.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or services change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Lucky through the official accounts listed on the Socials page.
